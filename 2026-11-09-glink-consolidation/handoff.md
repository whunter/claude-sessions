## Conventions to keep

- **Editing master:**
  - Read it as bytes and confirm that `csv.reader` followed by `csv.writer(lineterminator='\r\n')` reproduces the file byte-for-byte before changing anything.
  - Assert the old values before you change them.
  - Report how many lines changed.
  - The snippet is in `metadata-consolidation-status.md` and `tags_merge.py`.
- **Multi-valued lists** use `||` with no spaces (`tags`, `source_tiff_files`).
- **Page-level data** goes in separate files named `<identifier>_<thing>.csv`. Master points to them with `path: <relative path>`; paths are relative to `docs/`.
  - alt_text and visual_description for the sketchbooks point to `glink0010NN.csv`.
  - alt_text for 002128 points to `glink002128_alt_text.csv`.
  - Sketchbook `date_notes` point to `glink0010NN_dates.csv`.
- **Aggregated tags:**
  - For multi-image records, the `tags` cell in master holds the 15 most common page tags, counted by how many images carry each tag.
  - Images tagged "cover" or "verso" are left out of the counts, and ties are broken by first appearance.
  - The full per-image tags stay in the `_tags.csv` files.
- **Merged inputs** go to `docs/merged/`.
- **Image source:** `~/dev/dlp/assets/glink/media/scaled/glinkNNNNNNPPP.jpg` (536 images). Ignore `representative.jpg`.
- **Dates** use EDTF-style values (`1934/1937`, `195X`, `XXXX-07-21`).
